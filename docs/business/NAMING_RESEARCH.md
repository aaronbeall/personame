# Naming research: Personame and alternatives

Checked October 28, 2026. Preliminary public-use and domain screening, not trademark clearance. No names or domains have been purchased, reserved, or adopted.

## Recommendation

**Avoid Personome and Personica:** both already appear on personality products with substantial category overlap. **Personame is worth investigating further if we love it**, but an established branding studio uses the exact name, and the `.com` and `.app` are registered.

For a new direction, prioritize **Personafetti** for the whimsical social brand, **Personori** for a broader consumer/business identity, and **Personosaic** for the collection-of-selves concept. These are creative recommendations, not cleared marks.

### Trademark evidence and limits

Public web searches surfaced third-party PERSONICA trademark records, including [US application serial 99708330](https://www.trademarkelite.com/trademark/trademark-detail/99708330/PERSONICA) and [serial 90251800](https://www.trademarkia.com/personica-90251800). **These are leads, not verified current official statuses.** The official TSDR page did not expose the record contents in this research environment. No conclusion about registration, abandonment, ownership, enforceability, or freedom to use should be drawn from these indexes.

No comprehensive official-register search was completed for the originals or alternatives. An exact-name web search misses unindexed businesses and similar marks. The [USPTO's clearance guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks) explains why similar marks, related services, and use beyond the federal register matter. A domain being unregistered does not establish trademark rights.

## Creative shortlist

Exact-name searches did not surface an obvious relevant commercial brand for the six names below in this pass. That is a limited search finding, not proof of uniqueness. Suggested pronunciations and meanings are our creative interpretations.

| Name | Intended association | Strength for this product | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Personafetti** | Persona + confetti; per-soh-na-FET-ee | Celebrates many selves, sharing, playful discoveries, and the existing art/confetti language. Distinctive consumer personality. | Longer; can sound novelty-focused. Test whether “Personafetti Studio” or “Personafetti Insights” carries sufficient business credibility. |
| **Personori** | Invented persona-family name; per-soh-NOR-ee | Shorter, musical, broad enough for quizzes and audience insight. My strongest restrained option. | Meaning is less immediate; test pronunciation and spelling aloud. |
| **Personosaic** | Persona + mosaic; per-soh-noh-ZAY-ik | A person made of many facets; connects dimensions, archetypes, and the collection of selves. | Strong idea, less effortless spelling and pronunciation. |
| **Personolio** | Persona + folio/portfolio; per-soh-NOH-lee-oh | Suggests a growing personal collection and accommodates creative business tools. | May imply a portfolio or personal-branding product. Casual/noncommercial string use surfaced, so do not describe it as never used. |
| **Selvosaic** | Selves + mosaic; sell-voh-ZAY-ik | Moves beyond the crowded persona root while keeping multiplicity and visual richness. | Less immediately recognizable; a say-and-spell test matters. |
| **Personomio** | Invented genome-like cadence; per-soh-NOH-mee-oh | Closest to the sound and feel of Personome without being the exact word. | Still close to Personome and other persona-root names. Lower priority because an empty exact domain does not resolve similar-name concerns. |

Other directions were screened out: [FaceTale](https://getfacetale.com/) already occupies adjacent self-discovery territory; Personara and Personomia surfaced existing uses requiring further investigation; facetfolk.com returned a registered record. They are not recommended as availability-led choices.

## Brand fit and next decision

We need a name that sounds welcoming when a participant shares a result and credible when a creator pays for insight. It should accommodate whimsical authored quizzes without suggesting medical diagnosis or universal scientific personality measurement.

Suggested shortlist to test:

1. **Personafetti** — strongest expression of the current artwork and social experience.
2. **Personori** — strongest breadth across consumer and business contexts.
3. **Personame** — preserve the name we already like, contingent on clearance and an acceptable domain.

Before selecting, ask a small set of people to hear each name once, spell it, pronounce it from text, and describe the product they imagine. Include potential business buyers as well as participants. Test actual phrases: “I made this on ___,” “Discover your many selves with ___,” and “___ Insights.” These tests have not yet been run.

For the finalists, confirm registrar availability and renewal prices, check social/app-store names, and commission a similar-mark clearance search covering the intended launch territories and relevant software, entertainment/quiz, and business analytics services. Expand to official US and other jurisdictional registers as appropriate; do not treat a single exact-match or single-class search as sufficient. Resolve the name before buying major launch creative or changing production URLs.

This document recommends investigation and positioning. It does not authorize a purchase, public launch, or application rename.
